Active Watches

    📱 How it Works

    Our server polls the live myUNSW database every 2 minutes. As soon as someone drops a class, you get an instant lock-screen alert.

    🍎 iPhone / iPad Setup

    1. Open this site in Safari.
    2. Tap the Share icon (⎋) at the bottom.
    3. Select "Add to Home Screen".
    4. Open the installed app icon from your home screen and hit Track to allow notifications.

    ⚡ Features

    • No Sign-Up: Zero passwords or accounts required.
    • Closed App Alerts: You do not need to keep this tab open; your phone screen will light up when a spot opens.
